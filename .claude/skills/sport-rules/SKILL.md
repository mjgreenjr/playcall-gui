### MLB
ESPN sends every pitch plus bookkeeping plays, so `mlbContexts` walks all plays in id order and describes each one. Plays marked not announced are left unseen (silent on every poll). The catch-up limit applies to announced plays only.
- Title: "{Away} {away_score} · {status} · {Home} {home_score}", with the play's own scores (not the latest). Status is "{Top|Bot} {inning} · {balls}-{strikes}, {outs} out(s), {n} on", e.g. "LAD 3 · Top 6 · 1-2, 1 out, 2 on · ATL 1".
  - Plays before the at-bat ended (Start Batter, pitches) show the count; Play Results leave it out and show the outs and runners after the play. A third out shows just "Top 6 · 3 outs".
- ESPN's `outs` is the count AFTER the at-bat, even on its first pitch, so outs are tracked: pitches use the previous at-bat's outs, the at-bat's Play Result sets them, and a mid at-bat out (caught stealing, picked off, thrown out) adds one. Runners on pitches are the runners before the pitch; on a result, after it.
- At-bat results are the Play Results whose id ends in 99xxxx. Other Play Results (substitutions, steals) happen mid at-bat and their `outs` is not reliable.
- Body: ESPN's text. Pitches drop the "Pitch N :" prefix (e.g. "Strike 2 Foul"). Start Batter keeps ESPN's text ("{pitcher} pitches to {batter}").
- Announced: pitches, Start Batter, Play Results, and the half-inning transition.
- Not announced: pitches that end the at-bat ("Ball In Play", "Strike 3", "Ball 4"; the Play Result carries it), Start Inning, End Batter/Pitcher, the Stolen Base play (duplicated by its Play Result), and fielder substitutions ("Dubón at shortstop.").
- Half-inning transition: the End Inning play is sent as title "{Away} {n} · {next half} · 0 outs · {Home} {n}", body "Side retired"; the next half is Bot N after "Mid N" and Top N+1 after "End N". Left out when the game is over (bottom of the 9th or later, or top of the 9th or later with the home team ahead); the final event covers that.
- Urgency: normal for scoring plays, outs (the out count rose), runners reaching (the runner count rose), runner substitutions ("ran for"), pitching changes ("relieved"), and runners put out mid at-bat. Everything else is low.

### NHL
Flat `plays` list of discrete events: Face Off, Stoppage, Shot, Blocked, Missed, Goal, Takeaway, Giveaway, Hit, penalties, Period Start/End.
- Announced: every play except the silent types (Hit, Period Start, Period End; the faceoff and stoppage around a period boundary are still sent). Unknown play types are announced.
- Ordering and "is new": NHL play ids are NOT chronological, and `sequenceNumber` is only the play's position in the list (ESPN inserts plays late, in the middle, shifting later numbers). The list is in game order, so take plays in list order; a play is new when its id is not in `sent_ids`, never by comparing to `last_play_id`. Skipped plays are added to `sent_ids` too. `last_play_id` is still written (last id sent) for the log.
- Title: "{Away} {n} · {time left} {period} · {Home} {n}" with the play's own scores. ESPN's clock counts UP from 0:00, so `nhlClockLeft` converts it to time left in the period (regulation length is `format.regulation.clock`, 1200; regular-season overtime is 5:00, playoff overtime a full period). If it can't convert, show ESPN's clock as is.
- Body: ESPN's text; penalties append their length, e.g. "Timo Meier High-sticking against Jack McBain · 2 min Minor" (`type.penaltyMinutes`, `type.penaltyType`).
- Urgency: normal for goals (`scoringPlay`) and penalties, low otherwise.
- Catch-up limit 10.
- Power play: the side with the man advantage gets "PP" after its score in the title ("UTA 1 PP · 18:03 1st · NJ 0"), dropped once the power play ends. `nhlPowerPlays` walks the plays in order. ESPN's `strength` label is relative to the play's `team` (Power Play: that team has the advantage; Shorthanded: the other team does; Even Strength: none). Plays with no team (stoppages) and penalty plays keep the last known side. A goal that ends the power play is still marked PP; the next play is not. The body never mentions it.
- Not tracked: a live penalty clock or 5-on-3 vs 5-on-4.

### NBA, WNBA and cbb (shared rules)
Flat `plays` list: made shots, misses, free throws, rebounds, substitutions, fouls, turnovers, timeouts, violations, Jumpball, End Period. The clock counts DOWN (12:00 to 0.0, sub-minute values like "31.4").
- Ordering: play ids are not in game order (the id is the game id followed by `sequenceNumber`, both out of order). List order is chronological; "is new" means the id is not in `sent_ids`.
- Announced: every play except the silent types ("Offensive Foul Turnover", which repeats the Offensive Foul at the same second). Substitutions and timeouts are kept.
- Miss bundling (`nbaBundles`): a missed shot or free throw (`shootingPlay` and not `scoringPlay`) goes out in one event with the rebound right after it: body "{miss text} · {rebound text}", title and urgency from the rebound. Both ids are marked sent together. A miss that is still the newest play is held back until the next poll shows what follows it, unless the game is over; a miss followed by anything other than a rebound goes out alone.
- Title: "{Away} {n} · {clock} {period} · {Home} {n}" with the play's own scores and clock; period is 1st-4th, then OT, 2OT, ... cbb plays two halves: 1st, 2nd, then OT, 2OT.
- Body: ESPN's text on one line (some contain a newline). A turnover with no player in it ("shot clock turnover": ESPN sends only the team's id for these) gets the team's abbreviation appended: "shot clock turnover (GS)".
- Urgency: normal for made threes (`scoringPlay` with `scoreValue` 3) and fouls (type contains "foul", not "turnover"); low otherwise, including 2-point baskets, free throws and bundled misses.
- Possession (the GUI's ball marker) is not in ESPN's data for basketball, so `basketballPossession` infers it from the plays: made shots and last free throws hand the ball over, rebounds and turnovers say who has it, a shooting foul sends the fouled team to the line, jump balls name the team, and period ends leave it unknown until the next shot. Checked against 40 complete NBA and WNBA games, the team it said had the ball was the next one to shoot or turn it over 99.7% of the time (answering on 98.9% of those plays). Hockey has no marker: ESPN gives nothing to infer it from.
- Timeouts and fouls (GUI header, under each score): `GameStatus.AwayCounts`/`HomeCounts` hold, per team, `timeoutsRemaining` (ESPN's `timeoutsUsed` is for the current period only, so it is not used; the row has one circle per timeout the team started with, i.e. those left plus the team's timeout plays counted from the feed, filled for each left and empty for each used; for the NBA and WNBA in regulation the row is always 7 (`raiseTimeoutSlots`), since ESPN cuts `timeoutsRemaining` to 4 by the end of the 3rd: SAC @ LAC on 2026-10-21 had LAC at 4 left with two timeouts taken, and left plus taken gave 6). NBA and WNBA teams get 7 a game (checked offline against six 2026 preseason games: no team exceeded 7 timeout plays, 4 in the 4th or 2 in an overtime; a charged coach's challenge, "CALL STANDS ... charged with a timeout", always comes with the team's Full Timeout at the same clock, 4 of 4, so it is not counted twice; a challenge "retain their timeout" came with a Full Timeout play in 2 of 7 cases, so whether ESPN's live `timeoutsRemaining` gives that one back is open, see the capture) but at most 4 in the 4th quarter, so there the number filled is capped at 4 minus the timeouts used that quarter (`timeoutsUsed`), and in overtime they get 2 each period, so the row is two circles (2 minus the timeouts used that period); college teams get 4 a game plus one per overtime, unused ones carrying over (5OT with none used is 9), worked out from the timeouts taken rather than ESPN's remaining count (checked offline against 20 finished 2026 games: no team's team-timeout plays (`RegularTimeOut`, `ShortTimeOut`) exceeded 4 plus one per overtime, and every one of them carries a team id; the `OfficialTVTimeOut` plays (160) carry none, and are not counted; a coach's challenge whose text says "charged with a timeout" has no timeout play beside it in college (5 of 5) and is counted by `timeoutsTaken`; the college challenge's `type` is the opposite of its text, "(Overturned)" for "Upheld", so the text decides. Not checked live: college `timeoutsRemaining`, `bonusState` values), `fouls.teamFoulsCurrent` (fouls this period; for the NBA the team's offensive fouls this period are taken off, `dropOffensiveFouls`, since the NBA does not count them as team fouls: OKC @ SA on 2026-10-20 had three personal fouls and an offensive foul, ESPN said 4 and `foulsToGive` 1, the arena board 3; since 2026-10-22 `nbaFouls` takes 4 minus `foulsToGive` while that is 1 to 4, which survives ESPN retyping plays late, and falls back to the play count at 0, where the team is in the bonus and the number says nothing more; WNBA and college unchecked, left as ESPN sends them) and `fouls.bonusState` (NONE, else the team is in the bonus; DOUBLE shows as "Bonus", and for cbb as "Double bonus" with other states as "1-and-1"). NBA and WNBA line (`foulsText`, Mike's call 2026-10-22): the team's fouls while under 4, and "Bonus" when the team is in it (the other team has 4 or more); a team at 4+ that is not in the bonus shows neither. College shows the fouls, or only the bonus once in it. NBA only: when ESPN's counts are missing, or a team shows 0 timeouts left that the plays do not back up (`espnCountsUsable`; CLE @ PHI on 2026-10-22 sent no counts, then 0 left for both and DOUBLE bonus for both), a team with a retained challenge beside a timeout play takes its timeouts from the plays too (`withRetainedTimeouts`: in 43 games from 2026-10-20 to 25, quarters 1 to 3, a team with an earlier retained challenge had ESPN's `timeoutsRemaining` one below 7 minus the net timeouts in 34 of 40 readings, against 227 of 280 equal without one; the five refunds seen by ESPN were the exception; in overtime it counts out of the period's 2 and only for a challenge kept in that period, since ESPN starts overtime at 2 left whatever regulation used; BKN @ BOS on 2026-10-27, the first NBA overtime captured, 117-117 after 4, ESPN gave 0 used and 2 left for both at the start, then 2 used and 0 left to BKN after a kept timeout and another one, which it did not refund either; one retained challenge in 62 had no team, which `netTimeouts` cannot attribute); `countsFromPlays` rebuilds them from the plays: 7 minus timeouts taken (a challenge that retains its timeout is given back), the team's non-offensive, non-technical foul plays this period, and the bonus when the other team has 4; the two-minute bonus is not modelled. Shown only while the game is in play. ESPN also gives `foulsToGive` (not used). NFL shows timeouts only ("●●○", no fouls): `footballCounts` takes 3 minus ESPN's `timeoutsUsed`, which counts the current half (it is 0 again by the end of the 2nd quarter, so halftime shows 3 each) and runs a play or two ahead of the numbered timeout plays ("Timeout #2 by PHI at 01:06."; checked over 9 games, they agree). Overtime is 2 each (ESPN's counter resets for it; seen in NYG @ WSH, and BUF @ LAR on 2026-10-12, where the header flipped to OT 10:00 with `timeoutsUsed` 0 about 1:40 after the End of Regulation play, a timeout each took one circle and the circles matched; PIT @ TB on 2026-10-18 went to OT too, and Mike saw the header and circles look right live). ESPN sends no coin toss play: the first OT play is the kickoff, then the first snap about 50 seconds later. College football shows timeouts too (`collegeCounts`), from ESPN's `timeoutsUsed`, which is a running total of charged timeouts for the whole game (up to 6, 7 with overtime; it does not reset at halftime, and an official timeout is not counted, though ESPN files it under a team too, so a count from the plays was right for the first half only about 80% of the time). Three a half: in the first half 3 minus the counter, at the halftime status three each, and in the second half 3 minus (the counter minus the first half's total). The first half's total is seen by the running poller while period 1 or 2 is in play (kept per side, `Poller.collegeFirstHalf`, and saved by the GUI in `first_half.json` in the cache folder, per game id and kept a week, `HalfStore`; a restart in the 3rd or 4th quarter takes it back only if it was saved at halftime, since one saved while the half was in play can be short). A run that starts in the 3rd or 4th quarter without a saved total has none, so it shows no circles, unless neither team has used a timeout (then it is 0 for both, kept for later); one side with none and the other with some is unknown too. Overtime has one circle per team per period (`collegeOvertime`; see the message-contract skill). Checked in DEL @ MTSU and FIU @ JXST on 2026-10-13 (see above), where the counter named the right team about 10 seconds before the timeout play arrived. The CLI feed's HEADER line shows the same "Timeouts" text.
- Catch-up limit 10. cbb is assumed to match and has not been tested against a live feed. No shot-clock tracking (ruled out).
